# Experimental scope

The protocol intervention is only `toDst.GetNextHop() == src` to `toDst.GetNextHop() == src && toDst.GetFlag() != INVALID`. Observer instrumentation and diagnostic friend declarations are separate. The no-guard variant is deliberately unsafe and only tests detector sensitivity.

The ensemble contains six fixed trajectories, two mobility families and three historical traces per family, with 25 nodes and 20 within-environment stock/patch pairs at RngRun 101–120. RNG seed is 20261030; Wi-Fi streams begin at 1000 and AODV at 2000. Application schedules are deterministic, five flows beginning at 60+0.17*i s, 10 packets/s, ending at 180 s, with 5985 offered packets and observation to 210 s. Matched exogenous inputs do not guarantee identical endogenous RNG consumption. The sample budget was fixed for descriptive precision, not powered noninferiority. Between-environment pooling is not an independent n=120 design. All negative and zero outcomes are retained.

Control-cost fields prefixed app_window refer to [60,180) s; other fields cover [0,210) s. Airtime is a sum of transmitted PPDU durations, not channel busy time. Paired Student-t intervals use 19 degrees of freedom. Percentile bootstrap uses 10000 resamples with a fixed analysis RNG seed. Intervals are descriptive and unadjusted for multiple environments/metrics.

Two local handler programs test 99 inputs per variant. The transition program additionally calls the real RecvReply handler and rechecks the VALID guard. These are handler obligations, not a topology-wide proof. Actual initialization callback order and non-purging state snapshots are recorded; they are not complete global scheduler histories.

There are 108 controlled temporal runs, 78 natural retained-state sensitivity runs and nine natural concurrent-source runs. Controlled runs restore a destination at 25 s and observe until 90 s; effective routing-table bad-link lifetime is explicitly configured. Natural runs have no artificial invalidation/restoration, use the original retained mobility and HELLO=true, and observe until 100 s. Natural retained-state qualification is 72/78; the six retention=5 s cases are negative controls. Combined retained/concurrent qualification is 9/9. Three RNG substreams in a fixed fixture are not independent topologies.

One-factor settings vary retention, retries, queue capacity, queue lifetime and traffic duration. RreqRetries varies independently of BlackListTimeout=5.6 s. Recovery is a retrospective five-consecutive-generation-window >=80% eventual delivery criterion, with a stricter <=1 s delivery version. Two-second tasks cannot meet the five-second assessability requirement. No arrival is right-censored at the observation horizon.

Synchronous routing-table snapshots monitor destination 10.1.1.4. The analysis distinguishes any instantaneous snapshot cycle from final-state-at-timestamp positive-duration cycles; durations are last-observation-carried-forward estimates, not continuous observations. INVALID→IN_SEARCH→VALID lineage stops at true absence.

Strict return detection compares node, IPv4 source/destination/protocol/identification, application flow and sequence. Non-loopback TX must be at a strictly earlier printed decimal timestamp, with a higher TTL than RX. Packet UID is auxiliary evidence. The observer covers logged study UDP data across observed nodes, not all protocols or unrecorded traffic; UDP source ports, payload hashes and fragmentation offsets are not recorded. All 195 original source/main-flow sequence sets remain unchanged by the stricter predicate. All three unsafe baselines have 32 returned identities.
